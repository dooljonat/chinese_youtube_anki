我对篮球很感___趣。What character fills the blank and why?	"兴 — the fixed expression is 感兴趣 (gǎn xìngqù) meaning ""to be interested in."" 对+topic+感兴趣 = to be interested in something."
我写了一本书_____篮球。(对 or 关于?) Which is correct and why?	"关于 — 关于 means ""about/concerning"" for topics of discussion. 对 implies physical/emotional orientation toward something. A book is ""about"" a topic not ""toward"" it."

In Chinese does TIME come before or after the verb?	Time comes BEFORE the verb — e.g. 我去年在高中家教 (last year comes before 在高中家教)
In Chinese does PLACE come before or after the verb?	Place comes BEFORE the verb — e.g. 我在成都学中文 (在成都 comes before 学中文) 
Where does 在 go when showing a progressive action (currently doing something)?	在 comes BEFORE the verb — e.g. 我在学中文 (I am currently learning Chinese)
"For ""two"" before a measure word do you use 二 or 两?"	Use 两 — e.g. 两个星期 (two weeks) 两块钱 (two yuan). 二 is used for counting or numbers in a sequence.
Which measure word does 星期 need?	星期 needs 个 — e.g. 一个星期 (one week) 两个星期 (two weeks)
Which measure word does 年 need?	年 needs NO measure word — e.g. 一年 (one year) 两年 (two years) 
Which measure word does 月 need?	月 needs 个 — e.g. 一个月 (one month) 两个月 (two months) 
"When asking ""what is X called"" where does 什么 go?"	什么 comes BEFORE 名字 — e.g. 你叫什么名字？NOT 你叫名字什么？ 
"To say ""X is Y"" with adjectives do you use 是 or 很?"	Use 很 not 是 — e.g. 他们很好 (they are good). 是 links two nouns not adjectives.
